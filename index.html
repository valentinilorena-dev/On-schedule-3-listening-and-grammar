How to suggest, respond and ask for an update
PurposeLanguage
Suggest an actionHow about + -ing? How about asking the technicians?
Why don’t we / We could / We should + base verb · Why don’t we review the schedule?
-ing phrase + would be my proposal · Using a different phone would be my proposal.
RespondThat’s a good idea. / It’s worth a try. / That’s possible, but … / I’m not convinced.
Ask for an updateHow’s the campaign coming along? / How far are you with the packaging? / What’s the current status?
Give an updateWe’re on track. / We’ve hit a problem with the battery. / I haven’t made a final choice yet.
Clarify or questionSo what you’re saying is …? / I take your point, but is that really the case?
